What is Thetis?

Thetis by Old North is a youth development program that uses soccer, mentorship, and family engagement to help boys build the discipline, resilience, confidence, character, and purpose they need for life.

SoccerThe arena for growth.
MentorshipGuidance from positive role models.
FamilySupport for the whole journey.
Our philosophy

Soccer is the arena.
Not the destination.

We use the lessons of the game to develop the person beyond it.

Training becomes a setting to practice accountability, persistence, teamwork, and confidence—not simply to chase athletic results.
