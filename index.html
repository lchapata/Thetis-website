01What is Thetis?

More than a game. A brighter generation.

Soccer, mentorship,
and family.

Thetis by Old North is a youth development program that uses soccer, mentorship, and family engagement to help boys build the discipline, resilience, confidence, character, and purpose they need for life.

Express interest in Thetis ↗

Soccer is the arena for growth. Mentorship offers positive guidance. Family engagement supports the whole journey.

Our philosophy

Soccer is the arena.
Not the destination.

We use the lessons of the game to develop the person beyond it. Training becomes a setting to practice accountability, persistence, teamwork, and confidence—not simply to chase athletic results.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is Thetis only about soccer?

No. Soccer is the learning environment; character and personal development are the broader mission.

Who is Thetis designed for?

Thetis is designed for boys and the families who want to support their development. Specific age groups and enrollment details will be confirmed before launch.

Where is Thetis based?

Thetis is based in North Carolina. Exact program locations and schedules will be announced as they are confirmed.
